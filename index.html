Engineering reference

Useful equations and design reminders.

Air-side sensible capacity

Q = ρ × V̇ × Cp × ΔT

Use mass flow in kg/s, specific heat in kJ/kg·K and temperature difference in K to obtain kW.

Water-side capacity

Q = ṁ × Cp × ΔT

Water flow must be based on the correct fluid properties and operating temperature.

Duct sizing

A = V̇ / v

Actual design also requires pressure-loss calculations, fitting losses, duct roughness and acoustic checks.

Pump hydraulic power

P = ρ × g × V̇ × H / η

Determine the complete system curve and operating point before selecting a pump.

Refrigeration COP

COP = (h1 − h4) / (h2 − h1)

Use consistent refrigerant enthalpy values and the correct cycle state points.

Professional design checks

Check applicable ventilation standards, local building codes, fire safety, acoustic limits, condensation, filtration, energy efficiency and manufacturer operating envelopes.
